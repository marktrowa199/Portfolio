## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and hiring teams reviewing Niel Arthur B. Rocacurva for entry-level software engineering roles, especially work touching software, data, AI, and IoT.

## Product Purpose

Present Niel's background, project evidence, skills, and contact information so hiring teams can quickly understand his engineering direction and evaluate his work.

## Positioning

An early-career engineer with a practical foundation across software and data work, IoT robotics, and enterprise IT operations. The portfolio should let real projects and clearly stated contributions make that case.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing implementation uses Next.js 14, React 18, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS. Preserve its single-page architecture and current routes.
- Keep the dark and light themes, responsive navigation, resume preview/download, existing contact methods, and documented projects.
- Do not invent project claims, metrics, results, screenshots, or repository links.
- The portfolio shows exactly two projects. JobUp is an AI-powered job application assistant built as a full-stack web app (Python/FastAPI backend, Next.js frontend, SQL storage, OpenAI API, deployed on Vercel and Render), with a live demo at https://job-up.vercel.app/ and source at https://github.com/marktrowa199/JobUp. AGROSENTINEL is an IoT and computer-vision capstone for spinach leaf-necrosis detection with an automated fungicide sprayer. Netflix Data Analysis and InsightForge are intentionally excluded and must not reappear in the UI.

## Brand Commitments

- The portfolio should communicate: “An engineer who builds things.”
- The user requested a professional, recruiter-friendly modular construction metaphor. Keep it subtle and avoid a children's toy or Lego fan-site treatment.
- Keep the presentation clean and minimal, with slate surfaces and restrained teal/blue accents in both light and dark themes.
- Keep copy direct and preserve meaningful project evidence.

- `components/about/About.tsx` and `components/timeline/Timeline.tsx` document the BSIT degree, Concentrix internship, and capstone contributions.

## Product Principles

1. Let documented work lead the presentation.
2. State contributions honestly and distinguish them from team outcomes.
3. Make role, evidence, skills, and contact paths easy to scan.
4. Preserve useful functionality and accessible interactions across themes and screen sizes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The user requires visible keyboard focus, semantic controls and navigation, readable text and contrast, responsive mobile use, and reduced-motion support.
